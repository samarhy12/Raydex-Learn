Common questions

Do I need an account to learn?

No. You can open any course and start a lesson without signing up. Create a free account when you want your progress saved across devices.

I forgot my password.

Use the password reset page. We'll email you a link to choose a new one.

My progress didn't sync.

Progress syncs a couple of seconds after you make changes, and again when you close the tab. If you're offline it queues and syncs when you're back online — the status pill in the corner of the course shows what's happening.

Can I reset a course?

Yes. On the My courses page, each course with progress has a reset option that clears it on all your devices.
